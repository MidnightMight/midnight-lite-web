## Why I built it

[Your story: riding an electric unicycle (EUC) and wanting to see more from the EUC World app's ride logs.]

## How it works

You upload the CSV that EUC World exports. The app reads it in your browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere), draws the route on a map, and charts speed, power, battery and temperatures over the ride.
