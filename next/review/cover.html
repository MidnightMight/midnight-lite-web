midnight-lite.com v2

A site for your projects (portfolio), a YouTube channel and build logs, with a discussion space. This PDF shows every page so you can circle, cross out and comment.

How to review

  1. Each page of this PDF is one page of the site, shown at desktop width. There's a notes column on the right for handwriting.
  2. Every section has a pink tag (H1, P3…). Refer to it in comments, e.g. "H4: no newsletter, just YouTube subscribe".
  3. Pink boxes explain what a section is for, or ask you something. They won't be on the real site.
  4. Text in [brackets] and grey "photo" boxes are placeholders. Layout and structure matter now; wording and pictures come later.
  5. The decisions page (page 3) has check boxes: tick one per question or write your own answer.
